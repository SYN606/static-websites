Essential Travel Advice for The Americas

🏔️ High Altitude Preparation

Cusco and the Andes sit above 3,300 meters. Drink plenty of water and coca tea, and allocate your first day for restful acclimatization.

🎟️ National Park Passes

US National Parks like the Grand Canyon, Yellowstone, and Yosemite require permits and timed entry during peak summer months.

💳 Tipping Guidelines

In the United States, 18% to 22% tipping is customary for restaurant table service and taxi drivers. In South America, 10% is standard.
