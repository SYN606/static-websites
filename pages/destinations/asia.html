Asian Travel Advice & Customs

🌸 Peak Bloom Seasons

Cherry blossoms in Japan peak from late March through early April. Autumn foliage turns ablaze with crimson momiji in November.

🙏 Temple Etiquette

Remove shoes before entering Japanese ryokans and temples across Thailand and Bali. Cover shoulders and avoid pointing feet toward Buddha statues.

💴 Local Currencies & Cards

While major cards are widely accepted in cities, carry some cash for Japanese vending machines, street food markets, and temple donation boxes.
