Regional Travel Advice & Best Times to Visit

☀️ Best Time for Egypt & UAE

October through April offers comfortable temperatures ranging between 20°C and 28°C (68°F–82°F), ideal for temple exploration and desert activities.

🦓 Best Time for Safari Wildlife

The dry winter season from May to September makes wildlife easiest to spot around watering holes in Kruger and the Masai Mara migration peaks July–October.

👗 Cultural Modesty

When visiting mosques, religious complexes, and traditional souks, lightweight clothing covering knees and shoulders is recommended as a sign of respect.
