Terms of Service & Food Safety

Learn how we handle food hygiene, customer refunds, courier safety, and secure personal data processing.

Terms of Food Service

1. Ordering & Contract Formation

When submitting an order through Savoria, your request constitutes an offer to purchase culinary goods from the chosen independent partner kitchen. A binding contract forms once the kitchen marks your ticket as "Order Confirmed".

2. 100% Hot Food & Quality Guarantee

We guarantee all prepared hot food arrives above 60°C (140°F) in secure, tamper-evident biodegradable packaging. If an item is damaged, cold, or incorrect, notify our 24/7 concierge within 15 minutes of delivery for an instant replacement or a full credit refund.

3. Food Allergens & Kitchen Disclaimer

While partner kitchens strictly separate allergen-free items, commercial kitchens handle gluten, dairy, nuts, shellfish, and soy. Customers with severe anaphylactic allergies should contact the kitchen directly through the app before ordering.

Customer Privacy Policy

1. Information We Collect

We collect your delivery address, telephone number, email, and order history solely to execute food deliveries and optimize routing. Payment details are tokenized by PCI-DSS certified gateways; Savoria never stores raw card credentials.

2. Zero Data Selling

We never sell, rent, or monetize customer eating habits or location telemetry to external marketing agencies or third parties.
