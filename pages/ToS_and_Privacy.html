Terms of Service

1. Booking Agreement & Deposit Policies

By submitting a booking request on Voyage Vista, you confirm that you are at least 18 years of age and authorized to book on behalf of all travelers listed in your party. Standard tour packages are secured upon payment of a 30% refundable deposit. The remaining balance must be settled at least 30 days prior to scheduled departure.

2. Cancellation, Rescheduling & 100% Refund Guarantee

We take pride in our traveler-first flexibility:

  • 14+ Days Before Departure: 100% full cash refund or 100% future travel voucher with no penalty.
  • 7 to 13 Days Before Departure: 70% refund or 100% travel credit voucher.
  • Under 7 Days: Handled case-by-case; non-refundable supplier costs (e.g. non-changeable flight legs) are deducted.

3. Passports, Visas & Traveler Responsibility

While Voyage Vista provides comprehensive official documentation, invitation letters, and visa checklists, travelers are solely responsible for ensuring their passports possess at least six (6) months validity beyond the trip conclusion date, as well as obtaining any mandatory consular tourist visas and health immunizations.

Privacy Policy

1. Information We Collect

We collect personal details necessary to fulfill travel reservations, such as full legal name, passport information, contact email, telephone number, emergency contact, dietary preferences, and billing details. All financial transactions are securely processed through PCI-DSS compliant 256-bit SSL encrypted channels; we never store raw credit card numbers.

2. How We Share Information

Your personal information is shared strictly with verified travel partners (such as local boutique hotels, licensed safari guides, and high-speed rail operators) solely for the execution of your itinerary. We never sell, rent, or trade traveler data to external third-party advertisers.

3. Cookies & Local Storage

Voyage Vista utilizes functional browser local storage and cookies to remember your authenticated session, currency preferences, and itinerary builder draft progress. You can clear cookies or log out at any time.
